About

BinSense is an IoT-powered smart waste management system designed to modernize how we handle disposal in Olongapo City. By using real-time monitoring and data-driven insights, BinSense helps residents and local authorities manage waste more efficiently, reducing overflow and promoting a cleaner, greener community.
